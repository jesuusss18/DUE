Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Information We Collect

DUE stores your name, email address, hashed password, assignments, exams and reminder preferences.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to manage your account, organize academic activities and provide email reminders.

Google Services

DUE uses Gmail API to send academic reminders from its notification account. DUE does not access your personal Gmail inbox or Google account.

Data Storage

Application data is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted on Render. GitHub Actions is used to process scheduled reminders.

Your Preferences

You can enable or disable email reminders and change your notification preferences in Settings.

Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact duenotifications@gmail.com.
